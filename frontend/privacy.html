Privacy

AquaAI stores as little as it needs to. This page says what that is.

What we store

  • Your email address and sign in details, held by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. We never see your password.
  • The households you create: a name, a city location, household size, and a monthly gallon limit.

What leaves AquaAI

  • Questions you type into Ask AquaAI, plus that household's usage numbers, are sent to Google's Gemini service to write an answer.
  • A household's coordinates are sent to the National Weather Service to get a forecast.
  • Photos you choose in Report Waste are analyzed in your browser. They are never uploaded.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, and no selling or sharing data for marketing.
  • The meter readings you see are simulated, not collected from your home.

Your data

You can delete any household you created, or your whole account, from the app. Deleting your account removes your sign in and every household you created.
