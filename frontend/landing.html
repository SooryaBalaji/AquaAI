What AquaAI is, and isn't

Plain claims, backed by what is really running underneath.

Trained fresh per household

Every household gets its own seed, its own simulated meter history, and its own trained Isolation Forest, not one shared model.

Photos never leave the device

The Report Waste classifier runs entirely in the browser. No image is ever uploaded anywhere.

Real weather, real math

The forecast pulls a live 7 day outlook from the National Weather Service and corrects usage projections against it.

Works with zero setup

Runs on a local SQLite database by default. A cloud database is available if configured, but nothing depends on it.

Questions worth asking

Is the water usage data real?+

No, and this is stated plainly in the app. Household meter readings are simulated with realistic daily and weekly patterns, since a live feed would require a utility partnership. The AI models trained on that data, the weather forecast, and the photo classifier are all real.

Why these AI models and not something flashier?+

An earlier version used a TensorFlow LSTM on the backend for forecasting. That is a large dependency for predicting seven numbers, and a real risk of failing during a live demo. Isolation Forest and a browser side CNN do genuine, explainable work without that fragility.

Is my account and data secure?+

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text, and sessions use signed tokens. Worth knowing plainly: there is no password reset flow or login rate limiting yet. That is a reasonable scope for a student submission, not a claim of enterprise grade security.

Can I add my own household?+

Yes. Sign in, choose New household, pick a city and household size, and AquaAI trains a model for it immediately. Only you can see or delete a household you create.
