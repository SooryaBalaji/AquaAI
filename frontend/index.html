    Add data

    Log how much water a household uses. Every number in AquaAI is worked out from what you enter here. You only need one way to add data: enter days by hand, upload a CSV, or connect a Flume meter. Use more than one if you like. Days you add later replace earlier entries for the same date.

    Enter a day

    Adding data for

    Use the total for the whole day. You can get it from your water meter (today's reading minus yesterday's) or from your utility's online account. Bills usually list CCF, and 1 CCF is about 748 gallons. Saving a date you already entered replaces it.

    Upload a CSV

    One row per day with two columns, date then gallons, for example 2026-10-15,180. A header row is fine. Dates can be 2026-10-15 or 10/15/2026.

    Connect a Flume meter

    Pulls daily totals from a Flume smart water meter. Create a personal API client at portal.flumetech.com under Settings, then API Access, and paste its client ID and secret below with your Flume login. AquaAI uses these once to fetch your usage and does not store them. Flume limits personal clients to 120 requests an hour.
